Pulled this Craftsman out of a neighbor's shed. The cast iron top looked like the surface of Mars, the arbor bearings hummed like a washing machine full of bolts, and the fence... well, let's say it had *aspirations* of being square.

## What I did

1. Degreased everything with mineral spirits and a plastic scraper
2. Wire-wheeled the top (carefully — don't dig into the iron)
3. Replaced the arbor bearings
4. True'd the fence with a dial indicator and a lot of patience
5. Fresh paste wax on the top

## Lessons learned

Don't skip the bearing press. A dead-blow hammer and optimism will get you close, but "close" on a table saw is how you invent new swear words.
