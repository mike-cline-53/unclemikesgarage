Hey folks — welcome to **Uncle Mike's Garage**.

This is where I share what I'm working on out in the shop: fixing and refurbishing old things, woodworking, CNC jobs, 3D printing, automotive projects, and whatever else ends up on the bench.

## What's here

- **YouTube** — the vlogs and build videos
- **This site** — write-ups, notes, photos, and categories so you can dig into a topic

## Categories

You'll find posts filed under Woodworking, CNC, 3D Printing, Automotive, Restoration, and Shop Talk. More may show up as the shop grows.

## How this site works

Every post is a Markdown file in a git repo. When I push a change, the site rebuilds automatically. No fancy CMS — just words, pictures, and the occasional video embed.

Thanks for stopping by. Wipe your boots on the mat and hang around.
