Nothing teaches a CNC faster than cutting something you need *tomorrow*.

I started with soft jaws for the vise — aluminum blanks, a simple pocketing toolpath, and a lot of checking Z-heights. Then I cut a small shop sign out of scrap hardwood: "Uncle Mike's Garage" in a chunky 90s font, because of course I did.

## Notes from the run

- Probe everything. Twice.
- Climb mill when you can; the finish is worth it
- Soft jaws are the gateway drug to better workholding

If you're just getting into CNC, make fixtures first. Pretty parts come later.
